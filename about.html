OUR PROJECT

What is HealthCare+?

HealthCare+ is a Health & Hygiene Awareness project developed as part of a Community Engagement Project (CEP).

The project focuses on spreading awareness about basic healthcare services, personal hygiene, women's health, child health, nutrition, emergency awareness and preventive healthcare.

Our goal is to make health information easier to understand and more accessible to students, families and communities.

PROJECT PROBLEM & RESPONSE

Our Community Solution

This CEP project focuses on free community check-up camps, understandable health information in local languages, and health learning for rural women. HealthCare+ demonstrates these goals as a mobile-friendly website prototype, not a native Android or iOS application.

THE PROBLEM

Why Do We Need Health Awareness?

Many health problems can be reduced through basic awareness, hygiene and timely access to healthcare. However, people may not always have easy access to reliable and understandable information.

📚

Lack of Awareness

People may not know basic preventive health and hygiene practices.

🧼

Hygiene Challenges

Poor hygiene practices can increase the risk of several common infections.

👩‍⚕️

Delayed Medical Help

Lack of awareness about warning signs can sometimes delay professional medical care.

🌍

Community Need

Communities benefit from accessible health education and preventive-care awareness.
