WARNING SIGNS

Recognize a Medical Emergency.

Some symptoms may require immediate professional medical attention.

  • 🚨 Severe difficulty breathing or inability to breathe normally.
  • 🚨 Severe chest pain or pressure.
  • 🚨 Sudden loss of consciousness or unresponsiveness.
  • 🚨 Sudden weakness, difficulty speaking or other possible stroke symptoms.
  • 🚨 Severe bleeding that does not stop.
  • 🚨 Serious injury or major accident.
  • 🚨 Seizure that is prolonged or repeated.
  • 🚨 Severe allergic reaction with breathing difficulty or swelling.
  • 🚨 Any situation where a person's life may be at immediate risk.

DURING AN EMERGENCY

Stay Calm. Take Action.

Follow these basic steps while waiting for professional emergency assistance.

1️⃣

Call for Help

Contact emergency services and clearly explain what has happened.

2️⃣

Stay With the Person

If it is safe, stay with the person and monitor their condition until help arrives.

3️⃣

Follow Instructions

Follow instructions provided by emergency responders or qualified professionals.

4️⃣

Share Information

Tell responders about known medical conditions, medicines or allergies if this information is available.

BASIC FIRST RESPONSE

Help Safely Until Professionals Arrive.

  • Keep the area safe for yourself and the affected person.
  • If the person is unconscious but breathing normally, place them in a safe position if you know how to do so.
  • For severe bleeding, apply firm direct pressure with a clean cloth or dressing if possible.
  • Do not give food, drink or medicines to an unconscious person.
  • Do not move someone with a serious injury unless there is immediate danger, unless instructed by trained responders.
  • If someone is not breathing normally, call emergency services and follow the dispatcher's CPR instructions if available.

CHILDREN

Child Emergency Awareness.

Children can become seriously ill or injured quickly. Seek professional help whenever you are concerned about a serious condition.

👶 Breathing Problems

Severe breathing difficulty, blue or grey lips, or inability to respond requires immediate emergency assistance.

🌡️ Serious Illness

A seriously unwell child, especially one who is unusually sleepy, difficult to wake or not responding, needs urgent medical assessment.

🤕 Serious Injury

Major accidents, head injuries or severe bleeding should receive immediate professional attention.

☎️ Get Help

When a child's condition appears life-threatening, call emergency services immediately.

EMERGENCY CHECKLIST

Remember These Simple Steps.

  • ☑ Stay calm and check whether the area is safe.
  • ☑ Call 112 or the appropriate emergency service.
  • ☑ Clearly give the location and describe the emergency.
  • ☑ Follow instructions from emergency responders.
  • ☑ Do not give an unconscious person food or drink.
  • ☑ Stay with the person when it is safe to do so.
  • ☑ Take prescribed medicines or medical information if it can be done safely and quickly.
