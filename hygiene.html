WHY HYGIENE MATTERS

Small Habits, Big Protection.

Personal and environmental hygiene helps reduce the spread of germs and supports better health. Simple habits such as washing hands, keeping food clean, maintaining oral care and keeping surroundings clean can make an important difference.

01 / 06 · HAND HYGIENE

Clean hands,
better protection.

Handwashing with soap and clean water helps remove germs picked up during everyday activities.

WORLD HEALTH ORGANIZATION

Watch the handwashing demonstration, then follow the steps.

WHEN IT MATTERS

Wash at these moments

  • Before eating or preparing food.
  • After using the toilet.
  • After coughing, sneezing or blowing your nose.
  • After touching garbage or caring for someone who is sick.
  • Whenever your hands are visibly dirty.

YOUR STEP-BY-STEP ROUTINE

Wash hands with soap

  1. Wet your hands with clean running water.
  2. Apply soap and rub your palms together.
  3. Rub the backs of both hands and between your fingers.
  4. Clean fingertips, thumbs and around the nails.
  5. Rinse well with clean water.
  6. Dry with a clean towel or let your hands air dry.

02 / 06 · PERSONAL HYGIENE

Care for yourself
every day.

A personal-care routine can support cleanliness and comfort. Adapt it to your needs, available water and daily life.

WORLD HEALTH ORGANIZATION

Clean hands are one useful part of a wider personal-care routine.

A ROUTINE THAT FITS YOU

Daily care basics

  • Bathe regularly and wash after heavy sweating or activity.
  • Keep skin folds clean and dry.
  • Wear clean clothes and change them when they become sweaty or dirty.
  • Keep nails clean and trimmed; do not share towels or razors.

YOUR STEP-BY-STEP ROUTINE

Build a comfortable habit

  1. Start with clean hands and the water and supplies available to you.
  2. Wash your body and skin folds gently, then dry them well.
  3. Change into clean clothes when available.
  4. Keep personal-care items clean and do not share items that touch the body.

03 / 06 · ORAL HYGIENE

A healthy mouth,
a healthier smile.

Daily oral care supports comfortable eating and speaking. A dental professional can advise about your individual needs.

WORLD HEALTH ORGANIZATION · SCIENCE IN 5

Learn why oral health matters and ways to care for your mouth.

REMEMBER

Everyday oral care

Brush regularly, ideally twice a day. Use toothpaste recommended for you, clean your tongue gently, and limit frequent sugary foods and drinks.

YOUR STEP-BY-STEP ROUTINE

Care for teeth and gums

  1. Use a clean toothbrush and toothpaste suitable for you.
  2. Brush all tooth surfaces gently as part of your regular routine.
  3. Clean your tongue gently and rinse your toothbrush after use.
  4. Ask a dental professional about pain, bleeding gums or other concerns.

04 / 06 · MENSTRUAL HEALTH

Menstrual health
with dignity.

Menstruation is a normal biological process. Access to clean water, privacy, information and respectful support matters.

UNICEF INDIA

A community conversation about menstrual health and support.

CARE WITH COMFORT

Choose what works for you

People may choose different menstrual products. Follow product instructions and use available clean water and private space where possible.

YOUR STEP-BY-STEP ROUTINE

Manage products safely

  1. Choose a product that is comfortable and available to you.
  2. Wash your hands before and after changing or handling a product.
  3. Follow the product instructions for changing, washing or disposal.
  4. Ask a health professional about severe pain, unusually heavy bleeding, fever or other concerning symptoms.
Read UNICEF menstrual-health guidance ↗

05 / 06 · FOOD HYGIENE

Safer food,
healthier family.

Clean handling and safe preparation habits can reduce common food-safety risks.

WORLD HEALTH ORGANIZATION

Watch the Five Keys to Safer Food, then use the routine.

THE FIVE-KEY IDEA

Keep the food-safety chain in mind

Clean hands and surfaces, safe water, and keeping raw foods separate from ready-to-eat foods are useful habits.

YOUR STEP-BY-STEP ROUTINE

Prepare food more safely

  1. Wash hands and clean the preparation area and utensils.
  2. Wash fruits and vegetables with safe water; keep raw foods separate from cooked foods.
  3. Cook food thoroughly and use safe ingredients.
  4. Cover food and store perishable items safely; avoid spoiled food.
Read WHO food-safety information ↗

06 / 06 · ENVIRONMENTAL HYGIENE

Cleaner spaces,
healthier community.

Clean water, sanitation and safe waste practices depend on both household habits and community services.

UNICEF INDIA · WASH

See how water and sanitation support healthier communities.

SHARED RESPONSIBILITY

Small actions at home and nearby

Keep household water and food areas clean. Follow local waste and sanitation guidance and contact the relevant service about shared concerns.

YOUR STEP-BY-STEP ROUTINE

Care for shared spaces

  1. Keep household water containers and food areas clean.
  2. Use toilets and waste-disposal services safely where available.
  3. Keep children away from unsafe waste and standing water.
  4. Report shared sanitation or water concerns to the relevant local service.

WHEN TO SEEK HELP

Hygiene Helps, Doctors Diagnose.

Hygiene practices are important for prevention, but they do not replace professional medical care.

👩‍⚕️

Persistent Symptoms

Consult a qualified healthcare professional if symptoms continue, return frequently or become worse.

🌡️

High Fever

Seek medical advice for significant or persistent fever, especially when accompanied by other concerning symptoms.

🚑

Emergency

For a serious emergency, seek immediate professional medical assistance.
