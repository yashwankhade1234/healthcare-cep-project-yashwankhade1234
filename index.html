ABOUT THE PROJECT

Building Healthier Communities Together.

HealthCare+ is a CEP Project 2.0 initiative focused on improving health and hygiene awareness through simple, accessible and reliable digital information.

The project aims to support communities by providing basic healthcare awareness, hygiene education, women and child health information, medical camp awareness and a simple health-awareness assistant demo.
