Choose and store safe water

Learn how to follow local water-safety guidance and keep drinking water clean at home.

▶ Video✓ Guided steps↗ Trusted source
Start this lesson＋

Video by UNICEF India · English audio

What to remember

Water treatment depends on the source and local advice. Use guidance from your local water authority or health worker; one method does not fit every water problem.

Step by step

  1. Check local notices or ask a health worker about your water source.
  2. Use the treatment method recommended for that source and follow its instructions.
  3. Keep treated water in a clean, covered container.
  4. Use a clean utensil to take water out, and keep treated water separate from untreated water.

🧼HYGIENE · 3 MIN

Wash hands with soap

Follow a clear handwashing sequence and learn when clean hands matter most.

▶ Video✓ Guided steps↗ Trusted source
Start this lesson＋

Video by the World Health Organization · English audio

When should you wash?

Wash before preparing or eating food, after using the toilet, after coughing or sneezing, and whenever hands are visibly dirty.

Step by step

  1. Wet hands with clean running water.
  2. Apply soap and rub palms together.
  3. Rub the backs of both hands and between fingers.
  4. Clean fingertips, thumbs, and around nails.
  5. Rinse well with clean water.
  6. Dry with a clean towel or let hands air dry.

Comfort and cleanliness

Personal-care routines can vary with a person’s needs, available water, climate, and culture. Choose safe, practical habits that work for you.

Step by step

  1. Wash hands with soap at important times during the day.
  2. Change into clean clothes when available, especially after they become wet or dirty.
  3. Keep personal-care items clean and do not share items that touch the body.
  4. Ask a health professional about ongoing irritation, pain, or skin concerns.

What to remember

A varied diet can include familiar grains, pulses or other protein foods, vegetables, and fruit. Personal needs can differ by age, health, pregnancy, and culture.

Step by step

  1. Start with foods your household already knows and can access.
  2. Add variety across meals when available, including vegetables, fruit, grains, and pulses.

Daily care is useful

Get the right support

This lesson does not diagnose symptoms. For a serious or sudden emergency, use local emergency services or go to the nearest emergency department.

Step by step

  1. For urgent danger or a sudden severe problem, seek emergency help now.
  2. For a clinic visit, note when the concern began and what has changed.
  3. Bring a list of medicines or questions if you can.
  4. Ask the health professional what to do next and where to return if things worsen.
