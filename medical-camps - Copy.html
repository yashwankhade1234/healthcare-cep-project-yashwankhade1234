Basic Health Check-up

Basic health measurements and general health assessment.

🩸

Basic Screening

Basic screening services may be available depending on the camp.

👨‍⚕️

Professional Guidance

Get general health guidance from qualified healthcare professionals.

📚

HOW IT WORKS

Medical Camp Process

1️⃣

Camp Announcement

Information about the date, location and available services is announced.

2️⃣

Visit the Camp

Community members can visit the camp at the scheduled location.

3️⃣

Health Check-up

Basic health screening and consultation may be provided.

4️⃣

Health Guidance

Participants receive appropriate health awareness and guidance.

IMPORTANT INFORMATION

Your Health Matters.

Medical camps provide basic healthcare awareness and screening. They should not replace regular medical care when professional diagnosis or treatment is required.

⚠️

Seek Professional Care

Consult a qualified healthcare professional for serious or persistent symptoms.

Check Camp Information
