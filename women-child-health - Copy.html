MOTHER & BABY CARE

Maternal Health

Maternal health refers to the health and well-being of a woman during pregnancy, childbirth, and the period after delivery. Proper care during these stages helps protect both the mother and the baby.

🩺

1. Regular Pregnancy Check-ups

Regular antenatal check-ups help healthcare professionals monitor the mother's health and the baby's development.

  • Attend scheduled antenatal appointments.
  • Monitor blood pressure and other recommended tests.
  • Discuss any discomfort or health concerns with a doctor.
  • Follow medical advice and recommended vaccinations.

2. Nutrition During Pregnancy

A balanced diet provides important nutrients that support the mother's health and the baby's growth.

  • Eat a variety of fruits and vegetables.
  • Include pulses, beans, grains and suitable protein sources.
  • Drink safe and clean water regularly.
  • Take iron, folic acid and other supplements as prescribed.

🧼

3. Personal Hygiene

Good hygiene helps reduce the risk of infections and supports overall well-being during pregnancy.

  • Wash hands with soap and clean water.
  • Maintain personal and oral hygiene.
  • Eat properly washed and safely prepared food.
  • Keep the surroundings clean.

😴

4. Rest and Emotional Well-being

Physical rest and emotional support are important throughout pregnancy and after childbirth.

  • Get adequate sleep and rest.
  • Follow safe physical activity advice from a healthcare professional.
  • Talk to trusted family members about concerns.
  • Seek professional support if anxiety or sadness persists.

⚠️

5. Warning Signs During Pregnancy

Certain symptoms may require urgent medical assessment. Do not ignore serious or sudden changes.

  • Heavy vaginal bleeding.
  • Severe abdominal pain.
  • Severe headache or blurred vision.
  • Difficulty breathing, fainting or seizures.
  • Noticeably reduced baby movements later in pregnancy.

Seek urgent medical care if these warning signs occur.

👶

6. Postnatal Care

Care after childbirth is important for the mother's recovery and the baby's health.

  • Attend postnatal check-ups.
  • Follow medical advice for recovery and wound care.
  • Ask a healthcare professional for breastfeeding support.
  • Seek help for fever, heavy bleeding or worsening pain.
  • Discuss persistent sadness or emotional difficulties with a professional.

💚 Remember: Every Mother Deserves Care

Family support, proper nutrition, timely medical check-ups and access to qualified healthcare professionals can make pregnancy and recovery safer. Never hesitate to seek medical advice when something feels wrong.
